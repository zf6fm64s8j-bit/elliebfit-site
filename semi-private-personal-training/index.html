Semi-private personal training

Bring a partner, spouse, or friend. You train together, pay less than the solo rate, and still receive programming that fits each person individually.

Shared session, separate needs

You do not need to have the same experience, strength, mobility, or goals. Ellen can change the movement, equipment, range of motion, pace, or challenge for each participant while keeping the session moving together.

  • Personal coaching for two people
  • Exercises adapted independently
  • A shared appointment and training space
  • Equipment provided for in-home sessions
  • Package time usable in 15-minute increments

How pricing works

Each participant is charged three-quarters of the normal hourly rate. For a one-hour session, each person uses 45 minutes from their package. If one person pays for both participants, the session uses 90 minutes from that package.

Where sessions happen

Semi-private sessions can be held in-home across Ellen's Phoenix-area service area or virtually. Review the in-home service area.
