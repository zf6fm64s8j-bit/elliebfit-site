Personal training service areas

Above & Beyond Fitness brings personal training to homes across Cave Creek, Scottsdale and Northeast Phoenix. Virtual sessions are available anywhere.

In-home service area

In-home 1:1 and semi-private training are available in these areas, subject to your address and current scheduling:

Cave Creek
Personal training in your home, with equipment brought to you.
Scottsdale
Individual or shared sessions adapted to your goals and abilities.
Northeast Phoenix
In-home training scheduled around your address and Ellen’s availability.

Contact Ellen to confirm availability at your address. If you live outside the in-home service area, live virtual training is also available.

Virtual training

Virtual sessions are available outside the in-home service area and while traveling. They use the same live coaching and package rates as in-home sessions. Learn how virtual personal training works.
