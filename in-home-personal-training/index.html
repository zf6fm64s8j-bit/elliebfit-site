In-home personal training

Personal training without the gym commute. Ellen brings the equipment and builds the session around your goals, your body, the space you have, and how you are feeling that day.

What to expect

  • A free 20-minute consultation before you begin
  • Programming adapted to your experience, goals, and current abilities
  • Equipment brought to your home
  • Sessions booked in 15-minute increments
  • Coaching that can progress with you or work around a difficult day

Who it can fit

In-home training can work well if you are new to exercise, returning after time away, building strength or mobility, working around an old injury or joint concern, or simply more consistent when training comes to you.

Personal training can work alongside guidance from a doctor or physical therapist, but it does not replace medical care. Bring any movement guidance your clinician has given you.

Rates and service area

One hour is $90. Packages reduce the hourly rate to as low as $80, and package time can be used flexibly across in-home, virtual, and semi-private training.

Ellen serves Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, Phoenix, Cave Creek, Mesa, Tempe, Glendale, and nearby communities. See the complete service-area information.
