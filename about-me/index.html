About Ellen DeVore

Ellen DeVore is the founder of Above & Beyond Fitness and a NASM-certified personal trainer. She helps older adults, people returning to exercise and couples build strength and confidence with coaching that comes to their home or screen.

A lifelong interest in fitness, a career in helping people move

Born and raised in Kansas City, Ellen made personal training her career in 2010. She spent more than 13 years at AYC Health & Fitness in Prairie Village, Kansas, coaching clients one-to-one, with partners and in small groups. Her experience includes studio, in-home and virtual sessions, as well as teaching group exercise and leading wellness challenges at corporate sites.

Ellen moved to Arizona in 2023 and started Above & Beyond Fitness that October. Her independent practice brings that experience to people who want personal attention, practical coaching and a routine that fits their lives.

Adaptable coaching that feels approachable

Ellen has coached clients ages 8–92, learning to adjust each session to the person in front of her. She combines creative programming with patience, encouragement and a sense of fun. Whether you are rebuilding an exercise routine, training alongside a partner or working toward an activity you love, the starting point is your goals and current abilities.

Her training background includes older-adult fitness, golf-focused fitness and equestrian fitness. For clients who want to keep enjoying hobbies and everyday activities, those interests help make a workout feel connected to real life.

Experience and professional background

Ellen currently works as a personal trainer and group fitness instructor at The Valley of the Sun J, which she joined in 2024. Above & Beyond Fitness is her independent in-home and virtual coaching service.

As a certified PWR!Moves® instructor, Ellen also offers Parkinson’s-specific movement coaching. Personal training complements medical and physical-therapy care; bring any movement guidance from your healthcare team and discuss which training format suits your needs.
