Common questions

How does in-home personal training work?

Ellen brings all necessary workout equipment directly to your home and customizes each session around your goals, body, space, and how you feel that day. All you need is enough clear floor space to move.

What areas in the Phoenix metro area do you serve?

In-home personal training is available across Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, Phoenix, Cave Creek, Mesa, Tempe, Glendale, and nearby communities. Live virtual personal training is also available anywhere.

What are your personal training rates and packages?

Individual sessions are $90 per hour. Packages reduce the rate down to $80 per hour (5 hours for $435, 10 for $850, 20 for $1,640, or 40 for $3,200). Training time is sold by the hour and redeemable in 15-minute increments across in-home, virtual, and semi-private sessions.

What qualifications and certifications does Ellen have?

Ellen DeVore has 16 years of personal training experience coaching clients ages 8 to 92. She is certified through NASM as a Certified Personal Trainer (CPT), Corrective Exercise Specialist (CES), and Fitness Nutrition Specialist (FNS), with specialized credentials as a PWR!Moves® Certified Instructor for Parkinson's and in Female Functional Fitness.

Do I need to own any fitness equipment?

No equipment is needed. Ellen brings resistance bands, weights, and all necessary gear to in-home sessions. For virtual training, sessions can use whatever equipment you have available or bodyweight-only movements.
