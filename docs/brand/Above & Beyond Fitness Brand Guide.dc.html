02 — Logo
The Ascent mark
Three stacked chevrons — a mountain, a rising line, progress repeated. Opacity steps from 100% to 62% to 30%; never recolor the individual chevrons separately.
Primary — coral on white
Reversed — cream on sage
One-ink — for fax, embroidery, print
Lockup
Above & Beyond
Fitness · Scottsdale AZ
Mark height = cap height of both text lines combined. Gap between mark and text = 11px at 40px mark; scale proportionally.
Rules
Clear space on all sides = width of one chevron.
Minimum mark size: 24px screen, 0.35in print.
Never rotate, stretch, outline, or add a drop shadow.
Never place the coral mark on a coral or peach field — use one-ink or reversed.
Never re-typeset the wordmark in another face.

How to use it
60 / 30 / 10. Cream and white carry the page, sage and peach do the sectioning, coral is only ~10% — the thing you want clicked. Button labels on a coral fill are Ink, never white: white on coral is 2.92:1 and fails AA at any size.
No heavy darks. Sage deep is for small cards and type, not full-width bands. The brand reads light.
Never opacity for text. Use an explicit muted green (oklch(0.50 0.03 168)) so contrast holds on any wash.

06 — Voice & imagery
How it sounds and looks
Voice
Say
“Built around your body, your equipment, and how you feel that day.”
Don't say
“Crush your goals. No excuses. Beast mode.”
Short sentences. Concrete outcomes over adjectives — “get out of a chair without thinking about it,” not “transform your life.” Second person. No exclamation marks.
Photography
Real clients and real rooms — living rooms, patios, desert trails. No stock gym.
Warm natural light, golden hour where possible. Never cool or high-contrast grading.
Faces visible and unhurried; crop toward the top of the frame so heads aren't cut.
Corner radius 20–24px on all photo blocks.
Export JPEG ~1400px wide, quality 82, lazy-loaded.
