Parkinson’s-specific training

PWR!Moves® 1:1 and semi-private training

PWR!Moves® is Parkinson’s-specific movement training designed to help you stay strong, mobile, and confident in daily life. Sessions combine functional movement practice with strength, balance, agility, aerobic conditioning, flexibility, and cognitive challenges.

Ellen adapts every exercise to your abilities, symptoms, goals, and the kind of day you’re having. Train 1:1 or bring a partner, spouse, or friend. Either way, you’ll receive thoughtful coaching, individual attention, and a plan that meets you where you are.

You’ll be encouraged to do more than you thought you could in a fun, supportive environment.

PWR!Moves® training is designed to complement, not replace, guidance from your doctor, physical therapist, or occupational therapist.

What a session includes

  • Functional movement practice built around everyday tasks
  • Strength and aerobic conditioning
  • Balance and agility work
  • Flexibility
  • Cognitive challenges layered into movement

Ways to train

1:1
