Virtual personal training

A live video session coached in real time—not a recording. Train from your living room, hotel, or out-of-state home using the equipment you have, or no equipment at all.

How it works

  • Meet Ellen by live video at your scheduled time
  • Use your available floor space and equipment
  • Receive movement cues, alternatives, and feedback in real time
  • Adjust the session to how you feel that day
  • Use the same packages as in-home training

Useful at home and away

Virtual training can keep your routine consistent during travel, make training accessible outside Ellen's Arizona service area, or simply remove the time needed to drive to an appointment.

You do not need a home gym. Tell Ellen what equipment and space you have, and she will program around it.

Rates

One hour is $90. Packages reduce the hourly rate to as low as $80 and can be used flexibly across virtual, in-home, and semi-private formats.
